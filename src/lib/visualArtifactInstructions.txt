Argument format: use {kind:"argument",passage,premises,conclusions,reading}. Quote a continuous relevant passage. Premises are {id,text,quote,without} for a stated supporting reason or {id,text,unstated:true,without} for a genuinely necessary inferred assumption. "without" explains the specific consequence of withholding that premise. Use 1–8 premises, including at least one stated premise. Each of 1–6 conclusions is {id,text,quote,supports:[["p1","p2"],["p3"]]}. Each inner group needs all its members; different groups are independent alternative lines of support. Use 1–4 groups of 1–8 unique IDs pointing only to premises or earlier conclusions. IDs start with a letter, contain letters, numbers, underscores or hyphens, and have at most 32 characters. Every quote must be contiguous in passage; never present a hypothesis as a quotation. Distinguish loss of support from a claim being disproved.
