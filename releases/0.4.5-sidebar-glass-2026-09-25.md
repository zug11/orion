# Orion 0.4.5 — sidebar glass and freestanding mark

New note and its generation chevron share a translucent surface over the
existing native glass when Liquid Glass is enabled. A subtle rim and an extra
12% surface tint distinguish the control from the sidebar. Glass-off and
accessibility modes retain solid surfaces. Compact sidebars stack the actions
inside one outline.

Selected Home/Notes/Sources/Chat items, the Space switcher, and both dropdowns
follow the same glass toggle. Dropdowns have stronger frosting for legibility.
Glass surfaces use a uniform translucent tint, with no decorative gradient.
The New note chevron and generation menu stay hidden without a key for the
selected AI provider. Blank notes remain available.

The sidebar now displays only the teal/cobalt O with transparent surroundings
and a transparent central hole. The native app icon retains its corrected
full-bleed background for macOS masking.

The app identity and existing vault location remain `app.orion.knowledge`.
