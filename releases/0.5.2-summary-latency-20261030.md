# Orion 0.5.2 — writing and Space-summary maintenance update

30 October 2026. Apple Silicon, Medium transcription edition.

This maintenance build keeps ordinary autosave and reduces background Space-summary work while writing.

- Automatic summary validation and preparation wait until the window has had three seconds without a note edit.
- Incoming automatic summary results wait for the same pause before validation, publication, or follow-on requests. Stale results cannot replace the current overview.
- Unchanged immutable notes reuse their compact digests; changed concept labels and relationship hints invalidate affected entries.
- Delayed refreshes recheck freshness, and competing refreshes reserve ownership before preparation. A manual refresh does not cause a redundant automatic provider request.
- Autosave retains its 800 ms pause and five-second continuous-writing ceiling. Explicit summary refresh remains available immediately.

The application version remains 0.5.2. The distinct installer label is `0.5.2-summary-latency-20261030.m`.

## Validation before packaging

- 2,917 renderer tests passed, with one existing skipped test. Two editor-loading timeouts during a concurrent build passed both individually and in the complete four-worker rerun.
