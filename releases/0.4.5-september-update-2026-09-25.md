# Orion 0.4.5 — September UI update

This update brings the new glass, typography, and theme system into the signed
desktop app, with clearer daylight shaders and summaries for smaller Spaces.

## What's changed

- Optional native Liquid Glass joins the top bar and sidebar into one continuous
  surface, with shared tint opacity and blur controls. New note and floating
  controls follow the glass setting. Solid mode keeps a curved content boundary.
- Hanken Grotesk and Lora are bundled locally, with revised text sizing and a
  Sans serif / Serif choice in the note editor.
- The freestanding Imprint O replaces the old icon. Theme colours can update the
  in-app, Dock, and Finder icons; Original restores the packaged artwork.
- Light-mode atmospheres use paper-toned rooms and controlled daylight lighting.
  Mirage has sharper antialiased lens edges. Always dark remains available for
  the Home atmosphere independently of the rest of the app.
- Across this Space summarizes even one short note, with a local summary
  available without an AI key. Blank starter notes stay out of summaries.
- Short linked selections can request an AI-generated article title. New note
  generation also produces a title while preserving titles edited by the user.
- Search includes full note and source text with readable matching excerpts.
  Saved Chat citations survive editing and export. Media imports gain bounded,
  cancellable jobs and transcription improvements.

## Install

Download **Orion-0.4.5-shader-clarity-2026-09-25-Apple-Silicon.dmg**, quit Orion,
and drag Orion into Applications. This Apple Silicon build bundles Whisper
Small and preserves the existing Orion library and settings.

Native Liquid Glass requires macOS 26 or newer; older supported Macs use the
native fallback material. Finder icon updates require a writable app installation.

The Claude connector (`.mcpb`) and Codex plugin (`.zip`) are included in the app
and available separately below. This dated update retains app version 0.4.5;
the earlier release and its Medium edition remain available unchanged.
