# Orion 0.5.3 — word-count visibility and AI controls

10 November 2026. Apple Silicon, Medium transcription edition.

The word-count pill now appears only while editing. Clicking Done removes it,
and selecting text in reading mode does not bring it back. Extra spacing for
the pill also applies only while editing.

AI image and writing controls take priority over the count. Composers,
generation/progress/error docks and review controls hide the badge, keeping
Accept, Insert, retry and discard unobscured. The count returns when those
controls close without moving the document. Ordinary editing and selected-word
counts retain their existing behavior and cached paragraph counting.
