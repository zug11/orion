# Orion 0.6.7

Highlight a passage and choose **Visualise** to explore it in five new ways. Pick one directly, or let Orion choose an interaction that fits the text.

- **Cycle** lets you turn through the phases of a repeating process. **Linked views** lets you move two perspectives together with one control.
- **Argument explorer** shows how a conclusion depends on its premises. Withhold a reason or assumption to see which lines of support still hold, then bring it back.
- **Zoom across sizes** compares lengths on the same scale, from the smallest subject in your passage to the largest.
- **Deep time as one day** places the events in a passage on a 24-hour clock. Events near the end of a long history can be just seconds apart.
- In light mode the Chat orb has a pearl highlight, a pastel cloud and a pale rim, even with a dark custom colour. It follows system appearance changes live.

These visuals work in notes, Space Chat and standalone HTML exports. The Claude and Codex connectors accept the same visual documents. For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13.3 or later, with on-device Whisper Medium and signed in-app updates.
