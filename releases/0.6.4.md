# Orion 0.6.4

The cloud inside the voice orb now rolls against its glass shell, both at rest and as you move the mouse. This release also improves how voice research handles interruptions.

- Sweep sideways, vertically or diagonally across Jelly or Mirage Clear. The shell follows the sweep while the interior turns the other way and coasts as the pointer leaves.
- Stop a research worker while another lookup is pending and retry it in the same session. Sources that already arrived remain available. A successful retry clears the warning for the question it resolved.
- Parallel workers can begin with their own research questions, retain cited findings as the investigation grows and reopen an earlier passage to check its exact wording.
- Redirecting a worker to a different note or selection starts it with fresh evidence. Delayed ElevenLabs transcripts can use their original speaking context, and ChatGPT connection failures give more specific diagnostics.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13.3 or later. Includes on-device Whisper Medium and signed in-app updates.
