# Orion 0.6.8

Highlight a passage and choose **Visualise → Generate for me**. Orion can design an interaction around the passage, combining drawings, controls, and text in a custom layout. You can still choose a starting format yourself.

- **Play and adjust a drawing.** A drawing can now have moving parts and handles you can drag. In a mixing desk, for example, you can lower the monitor send while the audience meter keeps following the signal. Pause at any point or move through the animation with the keyboard.
- **Follow a process in an existing note.** Saved diagrams can play through their stages and trace the direction of a transfer. Linked views play together. These controls also work in a self-contained HTML export.
- **See how an argument reads with less evidence.** New Argument Explorer visuals can change whole paragraphs when you withhold a premise. Restore the premise to bring its supported reading back. You can inspect the source passage for the evidence.
- **Use the same visuals from Claude and Codex.** The bundled connectors accept custom layouts, animated drawings, and alternative argument readings through Orion’s existing artifact tool.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13.3 or later. This release includes on-device Whisper Medium and supports signed in-app updates.
