# Orion 0.5.0 — 12 October rebuild

Apple Silicon · macOS 13.3 or later

This rebuild includes the voice coordination, note-editing, autosave and signing fixes from [22a6c6e](https://github.com/zug11/orion/commit/22a6c6e466a0aea5f842e4117394d92a9548a18a).

## Changes in this rebuild

- Theme colours continue to follow the in-app O and running Dock icon. Finder uses the packaged icon, keeping the installed application signature intact after launch and theme changes.
- Explicit requests can start up to ten research workers without asking the user to assign a separate topic to each. Workers name their assignments, keep stable identities and retain independently accessible results.
- Voice can prepare focused edits and full rewrites in the current note through the same Accept/Discard and Undo flow used by Chat, without turning the edit into a research orb.
- A fresh voice session offers one neutral greeting. Open notes and text selections no longer invite unsolicited commentary; unfinished phrases receive more time to settle.
- Completed findings are grouped into brief updates with their original sources and coverage limits. All retained results remain reachable, with visible waiting/shared status and Read aloud controls. Switching orbs pauses the departing voice; Clear voice chat resets the transient conversation without deleting saved notes.
- Structured provider responses distinguish final answers from progress text, include actionable error codes and allow one bounded format-recovery attempt. Research preserves validated reading progress across that recovery.
- Autosave waits through short typing pauses and checkpoints during continuous writing. Unchanged Space, note and vocabulary objects are reused after saving, reducing unnecessary editor refresh work.
