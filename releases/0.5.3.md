# Orion 0.5.3

11 November 2026.

Orion 0.5.3 improves writing responsiveness and brings voice and text into one compact conversation.

### Changes

- **Smoother writing with autosave preserved.** Space summaries wait for a pause in typing, reuse unchanged note data, and defer incoming results while you write. Native connection polling runs away from the input thread, and the formatting toolbar avoids unnecessary redraws.
- **Reliable editing across windows.** A delayed library refresh can no longer mistake this window's own save for another window's edit or roll the editor back. Genuine competing edits still require a choice.
- **Word count while editing.** A translucent count sits at the bottom centre of the note. Highlight text to see selected words out of the total. Done hides it, and it steps aside while AI generation or review controls are visible.
- **Write before naming a note.** Title and subtitle are optional. Remove empty heading rows with Backspace at the start of the body, restore them with Enter, and keep that layout when reopening the note.
- **Voice and text share the space.** During a call, compact Chat opens on the voice scene without an empty message area. Sending a prompt reveals only the height it needs. Scroll down to expand the conversation; return to the first message and continue upward to shrink back to voice. Replies show a New reply cue without expanding the panel automatically.
- **Research results belong in the conversation.** Findings, citations, saved-note links and retry actions appear alongside chat. The worker queue and counts are removed from the interface. Voice options retains Stop research and Clear voice chat.
- **Image generation and layout improvements.** Updated image generation to GPT Image 2.6 at high quality, kept acceptance controls clear of the word count, and tightened the voice/chat layout to avoid overlapping controls and unused space.
