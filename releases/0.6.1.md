# Orion 0.6.1

Orion 0.6.1 brings project work into your notes and expands the ways you can talk, dictate and research within a Space.

## Work in your notes

- Create tickets and kanban boards with keyboard-friendly `/ticket` and `/kanban` commands. Add tickets inside a board and drag them between columns.
- Keep plain to-do lists separate from tickets. The dashboard adapts to the boards, open tasks and notes in each Space.
- Add optional ticket urgency and due dates. Infer tickets from open to-dos and longer notes without reviving completed work.
- Insert dates with `/calendar` and its keyboard calendar picker. The dashboard also recognizes dates locally in saved notes. Dismiss an inferred date without changing the original prose; calendars with no relevant dates disappear.

## Dictate useful structure

- One dictation can produce prose, checklists, tickets, a new board and editable tables together when the spoken material supports those structures.
- Optional AI cleanup removes hesitation and clear self-corrections while aiming to preserve meaning, uncertainty and emphasis. Original transcripts remain recoverable.
- Local Whisper dictation continues to work without an AI connection. Deleting a note also cleans up its orphaned dictation recovery sources.

## Chat, voice and research

- Keep separate conversations within each Space, with a collapsible chat-history panel that remembers its open or closed state.
- Connect with ChatGPT sign-in as an alternative to an OpenAI API key. Fixes cover completed response-stream assembly used by Chat and suggestions.
- Choose OpenAI web research through an API key or ChatGPT sign-in, alongside Perplexity. Research keeps source links and distinguishes cited summaries from original source passages.
- Choose ElevenLabs conversational voice, load voices from your account and use the voice studio. Continue the main conversation while research orbs work independently, with interruption support and quieter worker presentation.
- Expanded note-backed board and ticket tooling for Orion's local connectors.

## Download and updates

This release targets Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13.3 or later and includes the on-device Whisper Medium model. AI services are optional and use your configured provider connections.

Orion 0.6.0 and later can check for updates in the app. Installation remains your choice and waits for open windows to save. The release includes a signed updater archive and manifest alongside the DMG.
