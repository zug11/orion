# Orion 0.6.6

Highlight a passage in a note and choose **Visualise → Draw illustration**. Orion can draw the subject from your description, label its parts and show how it changes across stages. Select a part to read its explanation or ask a follow-up in Chat. **Save SVG** exports the stage you’re looking at.

- Ask for a visual in Space Chat and see it appear as the reply arrives. **Keep as note** saves the answer and its interactive visual together.
- Explore a calculator by dragging its chart or adjusting a slider. The results, numbers in the explanation and step-by-step working change together. Calculators can show conditional outcomes, draw flows in proportion to their quantities and show counts such as “12 out of 1,000.”
- Rescale a recipe by changing the number of servings, then add the ingredient quantities to your note as a shopping checklist.
- Inspect a figure’s supporting excerpt when one is provided. Visualise accepts whole-note selections and reads long passages in sections before generating the visual.
- Share a note as a self-contained HTML file. Its charts, sliders and illustration controls work offline. Claude and Codex can also insert a visual into a note through Orion’s MCP connector, with the same artifact validation and calculator range checks used in Orion.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13.3 or later. Includes on-device Whisper Medium and signed in-app updates.
