# Orion 0.4.5 — light-mode Mirage clarity

Mirage's daylight lenses used the broad glow mask from its night lighting as
an opaque silhouette. That feathered edge made light mode look lower resolution
although both modes rendered at the same canvas size.

Daylight now uses an antialiased edge tied to the render resolution, with the
refracted secondary light, rim shading, and reflections preserved. The existing
paper-toned room and restrained highlights remain. Dark output is pixel-identical
in the fixed-time comparison; the 600,000-pixel budget and 30 fps cap are unchanged.
No settings or vault migration is required.
