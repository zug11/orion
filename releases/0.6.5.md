# Orion 0.6.5

Highlight a passage and choose **Visualise** to turn it into an interactive illustration inside your note. Let Orion choose the format, or ask for a timeline, explainer, quiz, comparison, flashcards or calculator. Diagrams can show how a system changes across stages. Calculators let you adjust inputs, such as the interest rate and term on a mortgage, and see the results change.

- Type `/lead` in a note to create a contact or link someone already in that Space. After the first contact saves, **Contacts** appears in the sidebar. Open a contact to see the notes that mention them.
- Ask voice chat “What’s due this week?” or “Move the design review to Friday.” It can read the Space’s tickets and calendar, create tickets and all-day entries, move tickets between boards or columns, and reschedule deadlines. When a date comes from a sentence in a note, you can review the proposed change before accepting it.
- When inferring tickets, Orion can fill in descriptions and checklist steps from what you wrote. The review also shows grounded suggestions for deadlines and work already in progress.
- Search the Space switcher by name as you type, or use **Search by meaning** to see which note matched. Returning to a Space restores its view and scroll position.
- Continue dictating while browsing the same Space. The recording retains its original note and insertion point. Note details also record known writing contributions from Orion AI and external apps using MCP.
- Opening a shared `.orion` collection takes you directly to its entry note and preserves the originating Space name on first import.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13.3 or later. Includes on-device Whisper Medium and signed in-app updates.
